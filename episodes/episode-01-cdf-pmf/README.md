# Episode 01: CDF and PMF with LeBron's season-debut FGM

This seven-minute episode estimates the empirical distribution of LeBron James's field goals made in his first regular-season appearance from 2003–04 through 2025–26.

Field goals made is a discrete count, so the lesson uses a PMF, the discrete counterpart of a PDF, together with a CDF.
