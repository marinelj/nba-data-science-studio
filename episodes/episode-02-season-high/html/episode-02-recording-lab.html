Schedule sensitivity, density and data definitions

All-season results use actual team game numbers. For the exact 82-game population, use the 20-season subset. The additional 82-game equivalent is 1 + 81 × (G − 1) / (N − 1); it is a normalized position, not an observed game number.

A density histogram of normalized positions groups discrete observations. The bin area, not the height, gives relative frequency. This is not an exact continuous PDF of integer game numbers.

Age is measured at each season debut. “Season-high points” means the largest single-game PTS in that same completed regular season, not a season total. Predictions are made after the debut. Only age and debut PTS enter the model.

Sources: NBA player game logs and team schedules via nba_api, a third-party wrapper. Raw responses and request URLs accompany the lesson in data/raw and data/source_manifest.json.
